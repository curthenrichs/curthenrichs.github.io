## Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 6, 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how Curt Henrichs' Portfolio website, accessible from [https://curthenrichs.github.io/](https://curthenrichs.github.io/) (the "Site") and owned by Curt Henrichs LLC, handles your information. The short version: the Site is a static portfolio and collects nothing.

### What This Site Is

The Site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no user accounts, no forms, no purchases, and no backend of its own. I do not record anything you do on the Site.

### Data Collected

I do not collect, store, or process any personal data through the Site. As with most web hosting, GitHub may log technical information server-side (such as IP addresses) when serving pages. That logging is governed by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

### Cookies and Browser Storage

The Site does not set cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

### Analytics

The Site does not use analytics or tracking services of any kind.

### External Links

The Site links to third-party websites such as GitHub and LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and I have no control over what they collect. I encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

My blog, [Half-Built Robots](https://www.half-built-robots.com/), is a separate site with its own [privacy policy](https://www.half-built-robots.com/policies/privacy-policy/). This policy does not cover it.

### Email Contact

If you choose to email me, I will use your email address and message only to respond to you. I do not share, sell, or add your contact information to any mailing list. My email is a Gmail account, so messages you send me are also handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Your Data

You can ask me what information I have about you, or ask me to delete it, using the contact information below. Because the Site collects nothing, the only information I could have is an email you sent me, and I will delete it on request. If you are in the EU or UK and are not satisfied with how I handled a request, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

### Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has sent me personal information by email, contact me and I will delete it.

### Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, with the date above updated accordingly.
